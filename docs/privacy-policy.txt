Axios Workouts — Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 16, 2026

This policy describes how Axios Workouts (“the app”), published by KConSoft (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information on your device and, if you choose to create an account, through our cloud service providers. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are
Axios Workouts is a mobile fitness application developed and published by KConSoft. You can use the app without creating an account. An account is optional and enables cloud backup of your workouts when that feature is available.

2. Information the app stores
The app stores workout-related information you enter or generate locally on your device, including:
- Workout templates and schedules
- Exercise names and activity details
- Logged workout sessions (sets, reps, weight, duration, distance, and similar metrics)
- In-progress workout drafts
- App preferences needed for features such as workout timers and whether you dismissed the account onboarding prompt

Whether or not you have an account, this workout data is stored on your device first. If you do not sign in, or if you sign in before cloud backup is enabled, that data stays on your device unless you export or share it outside the app using features your device provides (for example, screenshots or system share sheets), which are outside our control.

3. Optional accounts
You may create an account with an email address, password, and username. You do not need an account to use the app’s core workout features.

If you create an account, we collect and process:
- Email address (for sign-in, account recovery, and confirmation emails)
- Username (display name you choose)
- Password (handled by our authentication provider; we do not store your password in plain text in the app)

When you are signed in, the app stores session tokens in your device’s secure storage so you remain signed in across app launches until you sign out or clear app data.

4. Cloud backup and sync
Cloud backup is an optional feature that may not yet be enabled in every version of the app. When it is enabled and you are signed in, your workout data may be copied to cloud storage associated with your account so you can access it from other devices and recover it if your phone is lost or replaced.

Until cloud backup is enabled for your account, signing in does not upload your existing workout history. Workout data on your device remains local.

If you sign out, your workouts remain on the device; only the sign-in session is cleared from secure storage.

5. Service providers
When you use optional account features, we use third-party services to operate authentication and (when enabled) cloud storage. Our primary provider is Supabase (supabase.com), which processes account and cloud data on our behalf under their terms and privacy practices.

Authentication-related emails (such as sign-up confirmation and password reset) are sent through Supabase’s email service. We do not use your email address for marketing unless we separately ask for your consent.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in this version of the app.

6. Network and third parties
The app uses network connections when you create an account, sign in, confirm your email, reset your password, or (when enabled) sync data. Your platform or distribution channel may also collect crash diagnostics, analytics, or update-related data according to their own policies.

The app may also open external links you choose to use, such as PayPal for optional donations, your email app for feedback, or your app store listing. Those services have their own privacy policies.

7. Data retention
Workout data is always stored on your device first. If you use the app without an account, or before cloud backup is enabled, we do not retain copies of your workout data on our servers.

If cloud backup is enabled and you are signed in, copies of your workout data may be retained in cloud storage associated with your account until you delete them or request account deletion as described below.

Your data is kept on your device for as long as you keep it in the app. It remains on your device until you:
- Delete individual workouts, logged sessions, or exercises inside the app, or
- Clear the app’s storage through your device settings, or
- Uninstall the app from your device

On Android, your device may include app data in automatic device backups if you have backup enabled at the system level. Those backups are managed by Google and your device settings, not by KConSoft.

8. How to delete your data
Because your workout data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time using the steps below.

Delete specific items in the app:
- Logged workouts: open a workout’s history and delete individual logged sessions.
- Workout templates: delete a template from your workout list (linked logged sessions for that template are removed as well).
- Exercises: remove exercises from the Exercise Library (this also removes matching exercises from your workouts and logs).

Delete all app data at once:
- Android: open Settings → Apps → Axios Workouts → Storage → Clear data (wording may vary by device).
- iOS: delete the app from your device (touch and hold the app icon → Remove App → Delete App).

Uninstalling the app removes all data stored by the app on that device.

Account and cloud data:
If you have created an account, you can sign out at any time from Settings → Account. Signing out clears your session from secure storage on the device; your local workout data remains on the phone.

If cloud backup has stored copies of your data, contact us using the email below to request deletion of cloud data associated with your account.

Request help with deletion:
If you need assistance deleting your data, email kconsoft@gmail.com with the subject line “Axios Workouts — Data Deletion Request.” We will respond to help you delete local data on your device and, if applicable, cloud data associated with your account.

9. Your choices
- Use the app without an account and keep all workout data local.
- Decline the optional account prompt and create an account later from Settings → Account.
- Sign out at any time; local workout data remains on the device.

10. Children’s privacy
The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at kconsoft@gmail.com.

11. Security
We rely on your device’s operating system protections for data stored locally. Account sessions use secure storage on the device. Cloud data is protected by our provider’s access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
